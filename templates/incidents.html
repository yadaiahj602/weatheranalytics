    🛡️ Standard Disaster Precaution Guides

    🌊 Flood & Waterlogging Protocol

    • Move to higher elevated terrain immediately.
    • Never attempt to drive through waterlogged subways or roads.
    • Disconnect primary electrical mains if water enters property.
    • Avoid open storm drains and manholes.

    💨 Severe Gale & Storm Protocol

    • Stay away from large trees, billboards, and metal hoardings.
    • Anchor loose rooftop items and shutter glass windows.
    • Keep flashlights and emergency power banks charged.
    • Do not park vehicles under aged trees or power lines.

    ☀️ Heatwave & Solar Radiation

    • Hydrate frequently with water and electrolyte solutions.
    • Avoid direct sun exposure between 11:00 AM and 3:30 PM.
    • Wear light, breathable cotton fabrics.
    • Seek shade immediately if experiencing dizziness or nausea.
