Emergency Response Protocols
🛡️ Standard Disaster Precautions & Safety Action Guide
☀️

Severe Heatwave Action

  • Drink water frequently, even if not currently thirsty.
  • Avoid direct sun exposure during peak diurnal hours (11 AM – 3:30 PM).
  • Wear loose, light-colored, breathable cotton attire.
  • Never leave elderly persons, children, or pets unattended in vehicles.
  • Keep oral rehydration solutions (ORS) readily available.
💨

High Gale & Squall Winds

  • Stay safely indoors away from glass facades and large unanchored hoardings.
  • Avoid parking motor vehicles beneath mature trees or sagging power cables.
  • Secure loose terrace sheets, solar water fixtures, and antenna arrays.
  • Avoid sea recreational activities or harbor boarding operations.
🌧️

Precipitation & Flash Floods

  • Move to higher ground if drainage overflow or waterlogging occurs.
  • Do not attempt to drive through submerged roads or underpasses.
  • Turn off residential electrical mains if water breaches property thresholds.
  • Keep emergency medical supplies, potable water, and non-perishables accessible.
